3. FFmpeg libraries (Linux VA-API backend)
-----------------------------------------
Project: FFmpeg
Website: https://ffmpeg.org/
Licensing: Normally LGPL-2.1-or-later, but builds enabled with GPL components
           are subject to GPL terms. Other optional components may introduce
           additional restrictions. Check the exact FFmpeg build.

Used by: Experimental Linux AMD/Intel VA-API backend only.
Libraries: libavcodec and libavutil.
Purpose: Allocate GPU hardware frames and submit them to the VA-API hardware
         encoder; compressed video packets are then passed to GStreamer.
